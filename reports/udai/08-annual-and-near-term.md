## 8.1 Tajika Varshaphal: the annual charts

### Method (Tajika Neelakanthi, *Samjna Tantra* and *Varsha Tantra*)

Nilakantha's *Tajika Neelakanthi* (1587) is the standard Indian text on annual charts. It has two parts. The *Samjna Tantra* gives the definitions: the Tajika aspects, the *deeptamsha* orbs, the five-fold strength *pancha-vargiya bala*, Ithasala and Ishrafa, and the sahams. The *Varsha Tantra* covers Muntha, the year lord and the Mudda dasha.

1. **The return moment.** The annual chart is cast for the moment the **sidereal** Sun returns to its natal longitude, **Gemini 6°57′33″**. It is cast for the place of residence; Mumbai is assumed here.
2. **Muntha.** Muntha starts in the natal lagna and moves one sign a year: natal lagna + completed years.
3. **The five office-holders (Panchadhikaris).** These are:
   - the Muntha lord
   - the natal (janma) lagna lord
   - the varsha lagna lord
   - the *tri-rashi pati*, the triplicity lord of the varsha lagna by day or night
   - the lord of the Sun's sign (day return) or the Moon's sign (night return)
4. **The year lord (Varshesha).** Neelakanthi's rule: the year lord is the **strongest of these five that aspects the varsha lagna by a Tajika aspect**. A planet that is strong but cannot "see" the lagna does not rule the year.
5. **Tajika aspects** are counted by sign:
   - 3/11 and 5/9 are friendly
   - 1, 4/10 and 7 are inimical (open)
   - 2/12 and 6/8 are blind
   
   An aspect is "live" within the mean of the two planets' orbs: Sun 15°, Moon 12°, Mars 8°, Mercury 7°, Jupiter 9°, Venus 7°, Saturn 9°. An applying aspect is *Ithasala* (the matter will come to pass). A separating one is *Ishrafa* (the matter has passed).

*Engine note:* pancha-vargiya bala below combines sign, exaltation, Egyptian terms (hadda), drekkana and navamsa, scaled to 20 units, using naisargika friendships. Neelakanthi's own friendship scheme differs in a few pairs, so read the figures as ranks rather than exact values. On his scale, above 15 is strong, 10–15 middling and 5–10 weak.

### A correction on sensitivity

It is often said that the varsha lagna "depends on the return moment, not the birth time". In fact the return moment is fixed by the natal Sun's longitude, and the natal Sun moves with the birth minute. A birth 15 minutes later puts the Sun 0.6′ further on, and the return comes 15 minutes later. **Error in the varsha lagna is roughly minute-for-minute with error in the birth time.** At about 0.3° of lagna per minute, ±15 minutes moves it about ±4°.

**Muntha is fully robust.** It depends only on the natal lagna sign (Capricorn holds from −43 to +66 minutes) and on age.

## 8.8 How to hold this

- **The near term has a clear shape.**
