# Ground rules for this reading

Astrology misleads most when it sounds certain, specific and personal. These rules apply to every chapter and every answer.

1. **Facts and tradition stay separate.** Planetary positions, dates and calculations are facts (Swiss Ephemeris, checked against Parashara's Light). What they *mean* is tradition, and the reading says so ("the texts associate…", never "you will…").
2. **Every claim is tagged twice.** First by how much birth-time error it survives (Robust / Moderate / Time-sensitive), then by how much the schools agree. Where the masters disagree, the reading says so instead of picking whichever answer sounds best.
3. **No cherry-picking.** With a dozen techniques something always "confirms". Supportive and difficult factors are weighed equally, and a claim counts as strong only when independent methods agree *and* it was stated before checking.
4. **Predictions are scored.** Specific claims are written down with date windows ([audit chapter](10-audit-and-contradictions.md)) and scored later. Misses count as much as hits. Readings about the past are tested against what actually happened, not adjusted to fit it.
5. **No fear.** There are no predictions of death, longevity, illness, accidents or disasters. Doshas are explained along with their cancellations and the masters' scepticism, not used to alarm.
6. **Nothing for sale.** Remedies (gems, rituals) are described as tradition only, never recommended. Anyone who insists you need a costly remedy is a red flag.
7. **The chart never decides.** Health, money, legal, career and relationship decisions rest on real-world information and qualified people. Astrology can be a lens for reflection, not the deciding vote.
8. **Watch for known traps:**
   - the Barnum effect (statements that fit anyone)
   - confirmation bias
   - self-fulfilling expectations, such as dreading a "bad" period
   - over-reading fragile factors, such as D60 or KP sub-lords
9. **"Unknown" is an allowed answer.** If the tradition is silent, split, or depends on a birth-time minute we don't have, the answer says so.
10. **It stops if it stops helping.** If any reading creates anxiety or pressure instead of clarity, that is a signal to step back.
