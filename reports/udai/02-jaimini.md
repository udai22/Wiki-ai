# 02 · The Jaimini lens: karakas, padas, argala and chara dasha

*Udai: 22 Jun 1999, 21:35 IST, Mumbai. Lahiri ayanamsa, whole signs. Sources: Jaimini's* Upadeśa Sūtras *(JS) as read by K. N. Rao, Sanjay Rath (SJC), P. V. R. Narasimha Rao and Iranganti Rangacharya, plus the Parashari arudha and argala chapters (BPHS ch. 29–31).*

> **How to read the tags.**
> - **[Robust]** holds across the whole plausible birth-time window (about −43 to +66 min, the span of the Capricorn lagna) and, broadly, across schools.
> - **[Moderate]** holds for the birth time, but the result depends on which school's rule you use, or it holds only for about ±15 min.
> - **[Time-sensitive]** flips within about ±10 min.
>
> Sutra numbers follow Rath's edition. Rangacharya's numbering runs a few sutras different, so treat numbers as approximate and the adhyaya/pada as firm.

Jaimini's system rests mostly on **signs** and **degrees within signs**, not on the rising degree. That matters for a chart where the birth time is uncertain: most of what follows is unusually stable.

## 1. Chara karakas: the seven- or eight-planet question  [Robust]

JS 1.1 defines the Atmakaraka as the planet "most advanced in degrees among the seven *or* eight" (*saptānām aṣṭānāṁ vā*). So the debate is in the sutra itself.
- **K. N. Rao and Rangacharya** work with seven karakas (Sun to Saturn).
- **Rath and PVR**, following BPHS 32, include Rahu as an eighth. Rahu's degree is counted backwards: 30° − 20°05′ = **9°55′**.

| Rank | Degree in sign | 7-karaka scheme (K. N. Rao) | 8-karaka scheme (SJC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Moon 28°32′ | **AK** Atmakaraka | **AK** |
| 2 | Venus 21°49′ | **AmK** Amatyakaraka | **AmK** |
| 3 | Saturn 19°34′ | **BK** Bhratrikaraka | **BK** |
| 4 | *Rahu 9°55′ (8-scheme only)* | — | **MK** Rahu |
| 5 | Sun 6°57′ | **MK** Sun | **PiK** Sun |
| 6 | Jupiter 5°12′ | **PK** Jupiter (the engine labels it PiK) | **PK** Jupiter |
| 7 | Mars 2°41′ | **GK** Mars | **GK** Mars |
| 8 | Mercury 1°28′ | **DK** Mercury | **DK** Mercury |

**A correction to the brief.** With Rahu at 9°55′, he sits between **Saturn and the Sun**, not between the Sun and Jupiter. The eight-karaka scheme therefore changes the middle of the list:
- Rahu becomes the mother-significator (MK).
- The Sun moves to father (PiK).
- Jupiter is the children-significator (PK) in both schemes.

**What both schemes share.** The top three (AK Moon, AmK Venus, BK Saturn) and the bottom two (GK Mars, DK Mercury) are the same either way. Those five are the karakas Jaimini leans on most.

**Why these are robust.** A ±90 min error moves the Moon by about ±0.75° and every other planet by almost nothing. The tightest gap is Mars and Mercury, 1°13′ apart, which no plausible time error can close.

**The karakas in brief**
- **AK Moon** in the 9th house, vargottama, waxing at 111.6° from the Sun.
  - SJC reads a Moon AK as a soul whose lesson is *care*: to nourish others without being ruled by moods.
  - Placed in the 9th, the soul's natural ground is dharma, learning, teachers and long journeys.
- **AmK Venus** in the 7th is also the Parashari yogakaraka for Capricorn (lord of the 5th and 10th). It is a strong "minister": career comes through partnership, aesthetics, finance, diplomacy and negotiation.
- **BK Saturn**, debilitated in Aries, is both lagna lord and guru-significator. Teachers and elders arrive through difficulty. Neecha-bhanga applies, because Mars (the dispositor) and Venus (lord of Saturn's exaltation sign) are both in kendras from the lagna.
- **GK Mars** in the 10th, vargottama: competitive work environments, with rivals who sharpen him.
- **DK Mercury** in the 7th, vargottama: the spouse-significator is dignified and placed in the house of partners.

## 2. Karakamsa and swamsa  [Robust]

The Moon's navamsa is **Virgo**, the same sign it occupies in the rasi chart (vargottama), and it sits about 1°52′ inside the navamsa, so it entered about 3.7 hours of clock time before birth and leaves about 2.9 hours after (+175 min, ≈00:30 IST on 23 Jun), when it also leaves Virgo itself. In D1, Virgo is the 9th house, which repeats the theme of dharma and teaching.

**Navamsa positions relative to the karakamsa (Virgo = 1)**

| From the karakamsa | Sign | Planets |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Vir | Moon (AK), **Saturn** |
| 2nd | Lib | Mars |
| 4th | Sag | Sun |
| 5th | Cap | Venus (AmK), Rahu |
| 9th | Tau | Jupiter |
| 11th | Can | Mercury, Ketu |
| 12th | Leo | empty |

**Swamsa readings from JS 1.2**
- **Saturn in the karakamsa.** The sutra gives *prasiddha-karmājīvaḥ*: one who becomes known through steady, sustained work.
  - Saturn here is in a friendly sign in D9, not the weak Saturn of D1. Commentators read this as recognition that comes from persistence rather than flair.
- **The Moon itself.** The sutra pairing a full Moon and Venus with *bhogī vidyājīvī* (one who enjoys life and lives by learning) applies partly: the Moon is strongly waxing but not full.
- **Aspects on the karakamsa.**
  - The Sun, in Sagittarius, aspects Virgo by **rashi drishti**. JS 1.2 links the Sun with *rāja-kārya*, work connected to authority or government. SJC extends these planet sutras to aspects; stricter readers apply them only to occupation.
  - Jupiter, from Taurus, falls in the 9th from the karakamsa and casts a **Parashari** 5th-house aspect onto it. Strict Jaimini (rashi drishti only) does not count that aspect, but Rath does use graha drishti within D9. Jupiter in the 9th from swamsa is the classic picture of fidelity to a guru and to dharma.
- **The 5th from the karakamsa** (Venus AmK with Rahu). Rangacharya and Rath read the 5th from swamsa for learning and mantra.
  - Venus points to poetry, arts and eloquence.
  - Rahu, which the karakamsa sutras associate with *yantra* (instruments, machines), points to technical or unconventional study.
  - Together they suggest a creative-technical mind.

**The 12th from the karakamsa: moksha and ishta devata.** Leo is empty in D9, so the sutras about a planet occupying it ("a benefic there leads to good worlds; Ketu there, *kaivalya*") are not triggered.

The schools differ on what to use instead:

| Rule | Planet(s) used | Deity (JS 1.2 / SJC correspondence) |
|---|---|---|
| Lord of the 12th (the usual SJC fallback) | Sun, which sits in the 4th from the karakamsa | Shiva, or Rama / Surya-Narayana |
| Planets aspecting Leo by rashi drishti | Mars | Skanda / Narasimha |
| | Venus | Lakshmi |
| | Rahu | Durga |

The Sun reading has a quiet confirmation: the Sun also aspects the karakamsa directly.

The 9th from the karakamsa (Jupiter in Taurus), used for the *dharma devata*, points to a guru or Dattatreya form. These are devotional orientations, not forecasts.

## 3. Arudha Lagna, Upapada and the padas  [Robust within the Capricorn window]

**How the AL is derived.** The lagna lord Saturn is in the 4th from Capricorn. Counting four signs again lands on Cancer, which is the 7th from the lagna. The exception rule (BPHS 29; JS 1.3) then moves the pada to the 10th from Cancer, giving **AL = Aries**, holding Jupiter and Saturn.
- Almost all modern teachers apply this exception: Rath, PVR and K. N. Rao.
- An edition that does not apply it would put the AL in Cancer (Mercury, Venus, Rahu). That reading is minority-only, but worth knowing.

The AL and every other pada depend only on the lagna *sign*. They therefore hold from **−43 to +66 min**.

**The padas**
- AL = Aries
- A2 = Gemini
- A3 and A9 = Taurus
- A4 and A10 = Capricorn
- A5 and A11 = Virgo
- A6 = Leo
- A7 (darapada) = Scorpio
- A8 = Aries
- UL (A12) = Leo

**Image versus reality.** Rath's phrasing is "the lagna is what you are, the arudha is what the world takes you to be (*māyā*)."
- **The reality: a Capricorn lagna holding Ketu.** Reserved, practical, self-effacing and somewhat detached. He does not naturally advertise himself.
- **The image: Aries with Jupiter and Saturn.** He is seen as an initiator with gravitas. He reads as principled, older than his years and responsible, sometimes as the one who carries the load.
- **Lagna lord in the AL.** Saturn, the lagna lord, sits in the AL. Rath reads this as a person who invests his real self in his reputation: what he appears to be is largely what he is, earned by effort.
- **Kendra relation.** The AL is 4th from the lagna. Because image and self are in a kendra relationship rather than 6/8 or 2/12, the gap between the two is one of *tone*, not contradiction.
- **The debilitated Saturn in the AL.** The classical warning is that early recognition can lag, or that he is seen as the underdog. With neecha-bhanga in place, the masters read this as standing that is built rather than given.

**Houses from the AL**

| From AL | Sign | Contents | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Ari | Jupiter, Saturn | Respectability and responsibility, both visible. |
| 2nd | Tau | empty; rashi drishti from Venus, Mercury, Mars, Rahu, Ketu | JS 1.3: benefic influence on the 2nd from the AL sustains the image and resources. Here the benefics (Venus, Mercury) are mixed with nodal colour. |
| 3rd | Gem | Sun | A malefic in the 3rd from the AL gives boldness and initiative (JS 1.3). |
| 4th | Can | Venus, Mercury, Rahu | Home and comforts are visible. Rahu here brings restlessness or foreign links around residence. |
| 6th | Vir | Moon (AK) | A benefic in the 6th is gentle and avoids open fights. The soul's real concerns stay partly out of public view. |
| 7th | Lib | Mars | Assertive public dealings, and the AL lord itself. The image is built through action and career (Mars is in the D1 10th). |
| 10th | Cap | Ketu; A10 and A4 | Ketu here: a work image that is technical, research-minded or quietly specialist. |
| 11th | Aqu | empty; aspected by Jupiter, Saturn, Venus, Mercury, Mars, Rahu | Heavy influence on gains (JS 1.3). |
| 12th | Pis | aspected by Sun and Moon only | Lighter influence on expenses. |

- **Gains versus expenses.** The 11th from the AL is far more influenced than the 12th, which Rath reads as income tending to exceed outflow over a lifetime.
- **Kendras from the AL.** Seven of the nine grahas sit in kendras from the AL. Only the Sun (3rd) and the Moon (6th) are outside, and both are in upachayas. This is an unusually *visible* chart: what he does tends to be seen. That includes the Saturn, Mars and Ketu signatures, not only the flattering ones.
- **The node axis.** Rahu is 4th and Ketu 10th from the AL. Rath links this axis to a recurring pull between home or roots and career identity, and to phases where public standing goes through resets.

**A10 (rajya-pada) = Capricorn, the lagna sign itself, and 10th from the AL** [Robust]. Self, home-image (A4) and career-image all coincide in one sign, so identity and work fuse. A10 in a kendra from the AL is a classic sign of professional standing (PVR).

**A7 (darapada) = Scorpio, 8th from the AL** [Robust].
- **The caution.** Rath counts an AL–A7 relation of 6/8 as a caution. Partners and business associates may present differently from how he presents, so agreements need explicit terms.
- **The help.** Jupiter and Venus both aspect Scorpio by rashi drishti, which is real benefic support for partnerships.

**Upapada (UL) = Leo** [Robust]. Leo is the 8th from the lagna and the 5th from the AL.
- **Trine to the AL.** A UL in trine to the AL is a harmonious placement in SJC teaching: marriage and public life support each other.
- **The 2nd from the UL** (Virgo) holds the **AK Moon**. In the UL sutras (JS 1.4) the 2nd from the UL shows how a marriage is sustained. A benefic there, and the native's own soul-planet, suggests he takes commitment seriously and holds it together himself.
- **Aspects on the UL.** By rashi drishti the UL receives Jupiter (Aries), Saturn (Aries), Mars (Libra) and Ketu (Capricorn).
  - JS 1.4 says benefic aspect relieves the malefic indications, and Jupiter supplies that.
  - Saturn and Ketu traditionally add seriousness or maturity to the bond, or ask for patience over timing.
- **The UL lord**, the Sun, is in Gemini, the 11th from the UL.

## 4. Argala (JS 1.1; BPHS 31)  [Robust]

**The rules.** The 2nd, 4th and 11th from a reference sign give primary argala (intervention); the 5th gives secondary argala. The 12th, 10th, 3rd and 9th respectively obstruct it, and an obstruction fails if it has fewer planets (JS 1.1, *na nyūna-vibalāś ca*).

**On the lagna (Capricorn)**
- **4th-house argala** from Aries (Jupiter, Saturn).
- Only Mars in the 10th opposes it: one planet against two, so the obstruction fails.
- Home, education, inner security and mentors actively shape the self. The 2nd, 11th and 5th are empty and unopposed.

**On the Moon (Virgo), which is also the AK**, the strongest of the three:
- **2nd-house argala** from Mars (Libra). The 12th (Leo) is empty, so it is unobstructed.
- **11th-house argala** from Mercury, Venus and Rahu (Cancer), three planets. The 3rd (Scorpio) is empty, so it is unobstructed.
- **5th-house argala** from Ketu, unobstructed in forward counting.
- The mind and soul-significator are well supported by resources, friends and networks, and by partners.

**On the AL (Aries)**
- **4th-house argala** from Venus, Mercury and Rahu, with Ketu alone in the 10th unable to obstruct it.
- The Sun in the 3rd gives a mild *pāpārgala* (malefic intervention): there is only one malefic, and the sutra wants several.

**Where schools differ: JS 1.1 *viparītaṁ ketoḥ*** (Ketu's argala runs in reverse).
- **Read as "Ketu counts backward"** (Rath):
  - From Aries, Capricorn becomes the 4th, so Ketu *adds to* the AL's 4th-house argala instead of opposing it.
  - From Virgo, Ketu becomes the 9th, so it *obstructs* rather than supports the Moon's 5th-house argala.
- **Read the other way**, the forward results above stand.

## 5. Jaimini and Parashari raja yogas

**AK–DK exchange** [Robust]. The Moon is in Mercury's sign and Mercury is in the Moon's sign, in **both D1 and D9**, since both are vargottama.
- **Parashari reading:** a parivartana between the 7th lord and 9th lord.
- **Jaimini/SJC reading:** a *sambandha* between the soul and the spouse-significator. Rath reads this as a partner who becomes integral to one's purpose.
- **The school difference:** strict Jaimini wants rashi drishti or conjunction, and Virgo and Cancer do not aspect each other by sign. K. N. Rao would weigh this less than Rath does.

**AK–AmK (Moon–Venus)** [Robust]
- **In D1:** a 3/11 relation with no sign aspect, so it is not a textbook Jaimini raja yoga. However, Venus is *disposited by* the AK, and the 3/11 is an upachaya relation.
- **In D9:** Venus in Capricorn is **5th from the Moon's Virgo**, a trine that SJC counts as a living AK–AmK link.

**AmK–DK conjunction (Venus + Mercury in the 7th)** [Robust]
- **Jaimini:** listed among the SJC raja yogas; career and partnership advance together.
- **Parashari:** the same conjunction joins the 10th lord (Venus) and the 9th lord (Mercury), a **dharma-karmādhipati yoga** in a kendra. It is the cleanest raja yoga in the chart, though Rahu's presence makes its results less conventional.

**Jupiter–Venus** [Robust]
- **Their relation:** 4/10 from each other, a mutual kendra.
- **Shared aspects:** both rashi-aspect **Scorpio (A7)** and **Aquarius (11th from the AL, the 2nd house)**. The two natural benefics therefore jointly protect partnerships and gains.
- **Other Jupiter links:** Jupiter, as PK, sits in the AL itself, and casts a Parashari aspect on Mars in the 10th.

**Karakas in kendras from the AL** [Robust]
- AmK Venus and DK Mercury are 4th from the AL.
- PK Jupiter and BK Saturn are in the AL.
- GK Mars is 7th from the AL.
- Rath and PVR treat the AmK in a kendra from the AL as a clear indicator of status through work.

**Lagna, Hora Lagna and Ghatika Lagna** [Time-sensitive]. These figures use sunrise at 06:02 IST (upper limb, with refraction).
- **The current positions.** The Hora Lagna is Virgo 22°45′ and the Ghatika Lagna is Virgo 2°22′, so **the AK Moon occupies both**.
- **The sunrise convention matters.** With Hindu sunrise (disc centre, no refraction: 06:06 IST, the convention used by the `astro/` engine and Parashara's Light), the HL is Virgo 20°43′ and the GL is **Leo 27°17′**, so the Moon joins the HL only. The double occupation holds only under the 06:02 convention.
- **The yoga.** Jaimini (and BPHS on special lagnas) counts planets that join the lagna, HL and GL as raja yoga. Here the Moon joins two of the three, not the lagna itself.
- **How long it holds.**
  - The HL stays in Virgo from about −45 to +14 min (−41 to +19 min with 06:06 sunrise), then becomes Libra (Mars).
  - The GL is Virgo only from about −2 to +22 min. Before that it is Leo, and Cancer (Venus, Mercury, Rahu) before about −26 min.
- Treat this as suggestive, not load-bearing.

## 6. Chara dasha

**Calculation (K. N. Rao)**
- **Direction of the sequence.** The sequence starts from the lagna and its direction is set by the 9th house. Virgo is in the *apasavya* group, so the dasha runs **backwards**: Capricorn, Sagittarius, Scorpio, and so on.
- **Length of each period.** It is the count from the sign to its lord, minus one. The count runs forward for Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Libra, Scorpio and Sagittarius, and backward for the other six.
- **Adjustments.** Add one year if the lord is exalted, and subtract one if it is debilitated. Capricorn loses a year to Saturn's debility.
- **Dual-lordship rules.**
  - **Aquarius → Rahu:** Rahu is conjoined with more planets than Saturn.
  - **Scorpio → Ketu:** Mars and Ketu are each alone, neither is exalted, and Ketu is higher in degree.
- **Robustness.** The sign sequence depends only on the lagna sign and planetary signs, so it is **[Robust]** for the whole Capricorn window.

### Cancer mahadasha, Jun 2026 – Jun 2036

In Rao's style, the dasha sign is treated as a temporary lagna.

**Why it emphasises career and partnership**
- Cancer holds **AmK Venus and DK Mercury** (and Rahu).
- From Cancer, the **AL (with Jupiter and Saturn) is 10th**, which K. N. Rao reads as a period when status and visible responsibility rise.
- From Cancer, **A10 is 7th** and the **UL is 2nd**. A sign containing the DK, with the UL 2nd from it, is one Rao regularly examines for formal commitment.
- Cancer's lord, the AK Moon, sits 3rd from it: effort and initiative are required rather than handed over.

**The transit backdrop**

**Antardashas (Rao: twelve equal parts of 10 months, counted backward from the next sign)**

- **Taurus, Apr 2027 – Feb 2028.** Learning, creativity, students; A3 and A9 are here.
- **Aries, Feb 2028 – Dec 2028.** The AL, coinciding with Saturn's transit of it (the exact return, 29 Apr 2029, falls in the next sub-period): tested reputation, and a clear chance to consolidate standing through responsibility.
- **Pisces, Dec 2028 – Oct 2029.**

**The next periods**

### Where the methods disagree  [Moderate]

**Scorpio's co-lord.** Some practitioners choose Mars over Ketu, for example those who default against the node or allow Jupiter's graha drishti on Mars as a strength rule. With Mars, Scorpio becomes 11 years, everything after shifts, and **Libra (2022–2031)** is the period running now.

**The SJC method.** As Rath and PVR teach it, the SJC method:
- counts each period by odd/even sign, not by K. N. Rao's "footed" groups;
- orders the antardashas differently.

For Capricorn lagna both rules still run the sequence backward. The durations change, however: Scorpio, Leo, Taurus and Aquarius come out differently.

**What is running now (Oct 2026) under each variant**

| Variant | Running now (Oct 2026) |
|---|---|
| K. N. Rao, Scorpio → Ketu | **Cancer** 2026–36 |
| K. N. Rao, Scorpio → Mars | Libra 2022–31, then Virgo 2031–33 |
| SJC-style counting, Scorpio → Ketu | Libra 2021–30, then Virgo, then Leo 2032–42 |
| SJC-style counting, Scorpio → Mars | Leo 2023–33, then Cancer 2033–43 |

**How to use this.** **Libra (the 10th house with Mars, 7th from the AL)** runs in every variant at some point in 2012–31. The 2030s are **Cancer (the 7th, holding AmK and DK)** or **Leo**, depending on the variant: the SJC variants put Leo in the 2030s, so the variants do not all agree.
- **Libra** shows career and competition.
- **Cancer** shows partnership, career through others, and commitment.

## 7. Lagna-sensitive factors

**Navamsa lagna** [Time-sensitive]. This is the main thing the birth-time uncertainty changes in this chapter.

| Birth-time offset | D9 lagna | D9 lagna lord | AK Moon / karakamsa | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before −5.5 min | **Pisces** | Jupiter (3rd of D9) | **7th** of D9 | The soul's lessons are worked out through partners. The AK aspects the D9 lagna by rashi drishti. |
| −5.5 to +7 min (recorded time) | **Aries** | Mars (7th of D9) | **6th** of D9 | Growth through service, competition and problem-solving. AmK Venus is in the D9 10th, which gives career prominence. |
| After +7 min | **Taurus** | Venus (9th of D9) | **5th** of D9 | Mantra, creativity, students. Jupiter rises in D9. |

The **karakamsa itself does not move**, and the swamsa readings in §2 hold in all three cases.

**Other lagna-sensitive factors**
- **D10 lagna** [Time-sensitive]: Sagittarius from about −9 to +2 min, Capricorn after, Scorpio before. It colours the career detail but not the A10/AL reading.
- **HL and GL** [Time-sensitive]: see §5.
- **AL, UL, A7, A10, argala and the chara dasha sequence** [Robust]: they hold from −43 to +66 min.
- **Which chara-dasha sign is "current"** [Moderate]: this depends on the school's rules, not on the clock.

*In the Jaimini lens, this is a chart whose public dimension is strong and visible, and whose soul-planet is a well-supported, vargottama Moon in the house of dharma. The difficult notes are a Saturn that must earn its place, a 6/8 between the AL and A7, and malefic aspects on the UL. Each is matched by a classical remedy within the chart itself: neecha-bhanga, Jupiter's and Venus's aspects, and Jupiter on the UL.*
