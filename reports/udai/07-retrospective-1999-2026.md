# 07 · Retrospective: what the traditions would have expected, 1999–2026

*Udai · 22 Jun 1999, 21:35 IST, Mumbai · Capricorn lagna, Virgo Moon in Chitra 2. Written 18 Oct 2026. Swiss Ephemeris, Lahiri ayanamsa, true node; all dates computed with this repo's engine (`astro.dasha`, `astro.transits`, `astro.rectify`) or directly with `swisseph`.*

A forecast can't be checked until it comes true, but a reading of the past can be checked now. For each period since birth, this chapter sets out what each school would *expect* to be prominent, so Udai can compare it with what actually happened. Every line has the form "tradition associates this period with…". None of them is a claim about his life. When the matches are consistently a few weeks off, the comparison becomes a **rectification tool**: Vimshottari dates slide about 24 days for every 15 minutes of birth-time error.

Two caveats:
- **Age shapes content.** A "7th-house period" at age 8 cannot mean what it means at 28. For childhood periods, classical readers route the results through parents, home and school.
- **Collective events are not personal confirmations.** The 2020–21 pandemic and the board-exam calendar were shared by the whole 1999 cohort. Only their *personal* texture counts as evidence.

Layers follow the master map: **[Robust]** holds for the whole Capricorn window (−43 to +66 min); **[Moderate]** means the dates slide; **[Time-sensitive]** depends on a few minutes.

**The factors that recur below [Robust].**
- **Rahu** is in the 7th (Cancer, Ashlesha), 1°44′ from **Venus** (5th and 10th lord, yogakaraka, AmK) and with **Mercury** (9th and 6th lord, DK).
- Rahu's sign lord is the **Moon** (7th lord, Atmakaraka) in the 9th, and its star lord is **Mercury**. By the rule that a node gives the results of its dispositor, star lord and companions, the Rahu mahadasha speaks for the 5th, 6th, 7th, 9th and 10th houses at once.
- **Ketu** is on the lagna.
- **Jupiter** (3rd and 12th lord) and **Saturn** (1st and 2nd lord, debilitated with neecha-bhanga) are in the 4th, Aries, which is also the AL.
- **Mars** (4th and 11th lord) is in the 10th with dig bala.
- **The Sun** (8th lord) is in the 6th.

## 7.1 Life map, period by period

Dates are at the recorded 21:35. The band gives each start date at +15 min … −15 min of birth time.

### Mars mahadasha balance: birth → 2 Oct 2003 (ages 0–4)
- Mars rules the 4th (home, mother) and the 11th (gains, the wider family) and sits in the 10th.
- For an infant, tradition reads the results through the household: the home, the parents' work and its momentum, and possibly a move.
- At birth Saturn was in Aries, 8th from the Moon (*ashtama Shani*, until 7 Jun 2000), and joined by transiting Jupiter.
- Only family stories can test this period, for example a move in 2000–03.

### Rahu mahadasha: 2 Oct 2003 → 1 Oct 2021 (ages 4–22)
Eighteen years of the 7th-house node, with its dispositor and star lord both tied to the 9th. Classically this period *pulls the native outward*: toward others, toward the unfamiliar, toward peers and outside influences rather than the family's default path. BPHS allows Rahu in a kendra, joined with the 9th and 10th lords, to give raja-yoga results. Most masters add that Rahu's gains arrive by roundabout routes, at the cost of restlessness.

**Ra/Ra · 2 Oct 2003 → 14 Jun 2006** (start 8 Sep … 26 Oct 2003) · ages 4–7
- The 7th, read for a child, is the arena of *others*: new surroundings, first sustained life outside the family. Typically this is the school start, and perhaps a move.
- Saturn moved into Cancer, the 7th, on 26 May 2005, and its 7th aspect falls on the lagna.

**Ra/Ju · 14 Jun 2006 → 7 Nov 2008** (start 21 May … 8 Jul 2006) · ages 7–9
- **Lordships:** Jupiter rules the 3rd and 12th, sits in the 4th with Saturn, and is 10th from Rahu, a workable kendra relation.
- **Classical tone:** many texts give Rahu–Jupiter a *guru-chandala* flavour, with teachers and moral frameworks prominent and sometimes contested.
- **Domains:** home, schooling, siblings or cousins, and distance.
- **Transits:** Sade Sati opened in Jul 2007, and the waxing Saturn square was exact on 27 Jul 2006.

**Ra/Sa · 7 Nov 2008 → 14 Sep 2011** (start 14 Oct … 1 Dec 2008) · ages 9–12
- **Lordships:** the lagna lord's period. Saturn rules the self (1st) and family resources (2nd) and is debilitated in the 4th. Rahu–Saturn is one of the "heavy" pairings in dasha-phala texts.
- **Overlaps:**
  - It sits almost entirely inside the **Sade Sati peak**: Saturn was in Virgo over the Moon from 10 Sep 2009 to 15 Nov 2011, exact on 2 Nov 2011.
  - The Rahu half-return over natal Ketu was exact **six days before it began** (1 Nov 2008).
  - An annular eclipse fell on the ascendant on 26 Jan 2009.
- **What tradition expects:** discipline, early responsibility, household or family-resource strain, and seriousness beyond one's years. In miniature, it is Raman's "setback that builds strength".
- **The question to ask:** do 2009–11 stand out in memory as the "heavy years"?

**Ra/Me · 14 Sep 2011 → 2 Apr 2014** (start 21 Aug … 8 Oct 2011) · ages 12–14
- Mercury (9th and 6th lord, vargottama) sits with Rahu in Rahu's own Mercury-ruled star. This is where Rahu "speaks most purely" through its star lord.
- **Domains:** analytical skill, tests and competition (6th), teachers and wider horizons (9th), and friendships (7th).
- **Transits:** the first Jupiter return was exact on 31 May 2011, just before the period opened, and the Western Saturn opposition came in Oct 2013.

**Ra/Ke · 2 Apr 2014 → 20 Apr 2015** (start 9 Mar … 26 Apr 2014) · ages 14–15
- **Classical tone:** node following node on the self–other axis. Texts call such periods unsettled, inward and abrupt.
- **Domain:** for this cohort it typically spans **Class 10 and its boards (around Mar 2015)**.
- **Transits:**
  - Rahu crossed the natal Moon on 27 Jul 2014.
  - Eclipses fell on Mars (Apr 2014) and opposite Jupiter (Oct 2014).
  - Exalted Jupiter passed over Venus from Sep 2014 to May 2015.
  - **Sade Sati ended on 2 Nov 2014**, when Saturn entered Scorpio, 3rd from the Moon, one of its best transits.
- **The questions to ask:** was the exam year inward and uncertain, or clarifying? Did relief arrive around Nov 2014?

**Ra/Ve · 20 Apr 2015 → 20 Apr 2018** (start 27 Mar … 14 May 2015) · ages 15–18
- **Classical tone:** the yogakaraka and AmK, within 2° of Rahu. Most texts would single this out as **the strongest sub-period of the Rahu mahadasha**: the 5th lord (study, creativity, romance) and the 10th lord (career direction) are both switched on.

**Ra/Su · 20 Apr 2018 → 15 Mar 2019** (start 27 Mar … 14 May 2018) · ages 18–19

**Ra/Mo · 15 Mar 2019 → 13 Sep 2020** (start 19 Feb … 8 Apr 2019) · ages 19–21

## 7.5 Which past events discriminate the D9 and D10 scenarios  [Time-sensitive]

Vimshottari dates slide about 1.6 days per minute of birth time, and a later birth moves them earlier. Each varga scenario therefore implies a date offset *and* a content signature.

| Scenario | Birth time | Boundaries vs recorded | Signature in the past |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D9 Pisces** | −18 to −5.5 min | 9–29 days later | AK Moon and Saturn in the D9 7th: relationship meaning centres on **Ra/Mo (2019–20)**. In Ju/Sa, partnership is weighted with duty. |
| **D9 Aries** (recorded) | −5.5 to +7 min | ±11 days | D9 lagna lord Mars in the D9 7th, and 7th lord Venus in the D9 10th: relationships key to **Ra/Ve** and **Ra/Ma**, with love and career entwined. Saturn in the D9 6th makes Ju/Sa about **workload and service**. |
| **D9 Taurus** | +7 to +19 min | 11–30 days earlier | Jupiter rises in the D9, so **Ju/Ju** feels personally expansive. Saturn is the D9 yogakaraka in the 5th, so **Ju/Sa** is creative, teaching or study-rich. The 7th lord Mars in the 6th brings friction in Ra/Ma. |
| **D10 Sagittarius** (recorded) | −9 to +2 min | +14 to −3 days | The D10 10th is Virgo with Rahu, and the Libra cluster sits in the D10 11th. Work is **advisory, teaching, analytical or networked**. In Ju/Sa, **income and network growth** come more than title. |
| **D10 Capricorn** | +2 to +13 min | 3–21 days earlier | Saturn, the D10 lagna lord, is exalted in the D10 10th with Venus and Mars. Work is **administrative, engineering or structural**. Ra/Ma brings a visible first job, and Ju/Sa a **clear step up in rank**. |

**The sharpest discriminators:**
1. **What Ju/Sa (Nov 2023 – Jun 2026) was about.** Saturn is the most time-sensitive planet in these vargas, so this one period separates four scenarios:
   - a rise in title points to D10 Capricorn;
   - income and network growth without a title points to D10 Sagittarius;
   - a creative or teaching flowering points to D9 Taurus;
   - a partnership carrying duty points to D9 Pisces.
2. **When the Jupiter mahadasha "arrived":** 7 Sep, 1 Oct or 25 Oct 2021, at +15, 0 or −15 min.
   - A felt change of chapter in early Sep 2021 favours the later-birth group (D9 Taurus, D10 Capricorn).
   - One in late Oct 2021 favours D9 Pisces.
   - The 10th-house double transit and Uranus on Saturn fall in the same months, so this boundary is well cross-referenced.
3. **The first serious relationship.** Its timing (Ra/Ve, Ra/Mo or Ra/Ma) and the partner's temperament test the D9 7th: Libra (aesthetic, diplomatic), Scorpio (intense, private) or Virgo (analytical, service-minded).
4. **The field chosen in 2016–17.** Engineering or administration favours D10 Capricorn. Advisory work, law or teaching favours D10 Sagittarius.

## 7.7 How to hold this

- **The marked years.** The traditions associate three stretches with the most marked themes. In date order they are:
  - **2009–11:** the Sade Sati peak within Ra/Sa.
  - **2015–18:** the yogakaraka sub-period.
  - **2020–23:** Saturn on the lagna, the Jupiter–Saturn conjunction, the mahadasha change and the Western Uranus contacts all converge.
- **If they don't match.** If those years were unremarkable and others carried the weight, that is evidence about the method, not only about the birth time.
- **Evidence.** As chapter 00 notes, controlled studies have not supported astrology's predictive claims. The fair test is to write down the turning points first, *then* read the period descriptions.
