# 07 · Retrospective: what the traditions would have expected, 1999–2026

*Udai · 22 Jun 1999, 21:35 IST, Mumbai · Capricorn lagna, Virgo Moon in Chitra 2. Written 18 Oct 2026. Swiss Ephemeris, Lahiri ayanamsa, true node; every date below was computed with this repo's engine (`astro.dasha`, `astro.transits`, `astro.rectify`) or directly with `swisseph`.*

A reading that only looks forward can't be checked. This chapter looks back. For each period since birth it sets out what each school would *expect* to be prominent, so Udai can compare that with what actually happened. Nothing here is a claim about his life. Each line is a statement of the form "tradition associates this period with…". Where the expectation and the memory agree, the method gains a little credibility. Where they disagree, that is useful too. When agreement or disagreement is consistently a few weeks off, it becomes a **rectification tool**: Vimshottari dates slide about 24 days for every 15 minutes of birth-time error, and the chapter ends with a checklist for exactly that.

Two caveats run through everything:
- **Age dominates content.** A "7th-house period" at age 8 cannot mean what it means at 28. The texts were written for adults, and below age 12 or so most classical readers take a period's results through the family: parents, home and schooling.
- **Collective events are not personal confirmations.** Everyone born in 1999 lived through the 2020–21 pandemic, and every Indian student of that cohort sat board exams in roughly the same months. Such events only count as evidence if their *personal* texture matches the period's description.

Layers follow the master map: **[Robust]** survives the whole Capricorn window (−43 to +66 min); **[Moderate]** means the dates slide; **[Time-sensitive]** depends on a few minutes.

### Mars mahadasha balance: birth → 2 Oct 2003 (ages 0–4)  [Moderate]
- Mars rules the 4th (home, mother, early nurture) and the 11th (gains, elder siblings, the wider family's network). It sits in the 10th, which some readers take as the father's 4th-from-9th sphere of standing and work.
- **What tradition associates with it:** for an infant, the period's results go to the household: the home, the parents' work and its momentum, and possibly a move. The sub-periods were Ma/Sa (to Apr 2000), Ma/Me, Ma/Ke (Mar–Aug 2001), Ma/Ve (Aug 2001 – Oct 2002), Ma/Su and Ma/Mo.
- **Transits:** Saturn was in Aries at birth, 8th from the Moon (*ashtama Shani*, until 7 Jun 2000). Jupiter was in Aries too, so the chart opens on its own Jupiter–Saturn configuration.
- **What to check:** this period can rarely be checked from memory. Family stories of a move or a change in a parent's work in 2000–03 would be usable.

### Rahu mahadasha: 2 Oct 2003 → 1 Oct 2021 (ages 4–22)  [Moderate dates, Robust content]
Eighteen years of the 7th-house node, its dispositor in the 9th, its star lord the 9th lord. The classical expectation is a period that *pulls the native outward*: toward others, toward the unfamiliar, toward peers, mentors and outside influences rather than the family's default path. BPHS treats Rahu in a kendra, associated with the 9th and 10th lords, as capable of raja-yoga results. Most masters add that Rahu's gains come in unconventional or roundabout ways, with restlessness and ambition as the cost.

- **Typical life domain:** school entry, a first school or a change of school, and possibly a move.
- **Transits:** Saturn transited Gemini and then Cancer (from 26 May 2005, 7th from the lagna, Saturn aspecting the lagna). Jupiter crossed the natal Moon on 21 Sep 2005.

- **Typical life domain:** home and schooling (4th), siblings or cousins and small journeys (3rd), and spending or distance (12th).
- **Transits:** Sade Sati began (Saturn entered Leo on 1 Nov 2006, briefly, and firmly on 16 Jul 2007). The waxing Saturn square fell on 27–28 Jul 2006, in both zodiacs.

**Ra/Sa · 7 Nov 2008 → 14 Sep 2011** (band 14 Oct – 1 Dec 2008 start) · ages 9–12
- **Classical:** Saturn, the lagna lord, rules the 1st and 2nd (self, family, speech, resources), is debilitated in the 4th, and is 10th from Rahu. A Rahu–Saturn period is one of the "heavy" pairings in most dasha-phala texts.

With the lord's debilitation cancelled, Raman's "setback that builds strength" reading fits here in miniature.
- **What to check:** what the household and family felt like in 2009–11, and whether this period stands out in memory as "the heavy years".

**Ra/Me · 14 Sep 2011 → 2 Apr 2014** (band 21 Aug 2011 – 8 Oct 2011 start) · ages 12–14

**Ra/Su · 20 Apr 2018 → 15 Mar 2019** (band 27 Mar – 14 May 2018 start) · ages 18–19

## 7.6 Which past events discriminate the D9 and D10 scenarios  [Time-sensitive]

The Vimshottari slide is about 1.6 days per minute of birth time, with a later birth moving dates earlier. Each varga scenario therefore implies its own date offset, *and* its own content.

**The sharpest discriminators, in order:**

   - A rise in title or authority points to the D10 Capricorn scenario (+2 to +13 min).
   - Income and network growth without a title change points to D10 Sagittarius.
   - A creative, teaching or study flowering points to the D9 Taurus scenario.
   - A partnership that brought duty or seriousness points to D9 Pisces.
   - Saturn is the most time-sensitive planet in these vargas, so this one period separates four scenarios.
2. **The exact month the Jupiter mahadasha "arrived"** (7 Sep / 1 Oct / 25 Oct 2021 at +15 / 0 / −15 min). The 10th-house double transit (Apr–Sep 2021) and the Western Uranus–Saturn contact (Jun and Oct 2021) cluster in the same months, so this boundary is well cross-referenced.
3. **The first serious relationship.** Its timing (Ra/Ve 2015–18 versus Ra/Mo 2019–20 versus Ra/Ma 2020–21) and the partner's temperament (Libra: aesthetic and diplomatic; Scorpio: intense and private; Virgo: analytical and service-minded) test the Aries, Taurus and Pisces D9 respectively.
4. **The field of study chosen in 2016–17** (Ra/Ve; Chara Libra/Aries; Jupiter over the Moon): engineering or administration versus advisory, law or teaching tests D10 Capricorn versus Sagittarius.

## 7.8 How to hold this

- The traditions associate the period from about 2009 to 2011, the period from 2015 to 2018, and the stretch from 2020 to 2023 with the most marked themes. Those are, in order, the Sade Sati peak in Ra/Sa, the yogakaraka sub-period, and the convergence of Saturn on the lagna, the Jupiter–Saturn conjunction, the mahadasha change and the Western Uranus contacts.
- If those years were in fact unremarkable, and other years carried the weight, that is worth taking seriously as evidence about the method, not only about the birth time.
- As chapter 00 notes, controlled studies have not supported astrology's predictive claims. This chapter is designed so that Udai can test the claims on his own life with dates in hand. The honest way to use it is to write down the turning points *before* reading the period descriptions closely.
