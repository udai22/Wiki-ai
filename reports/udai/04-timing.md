# 4 · Timing: the periods ahead, with uncertainty bands

*Udai · 22 Jun 1999, 21:35 IST, Mumbai · Capricorn lagna, Virgo Moon in Chitra 2. Written 18 Oct 2026.*

A dasha shows which part of the chart is "speaking", and transits show when the outer world answers. Neither one says what will happen. The texts treat a period as a weather system: it describes the climate, and the person still decides how to move through it. Every date below comes with a band, because the birth time could be off by a few minutes either way.

---

## 4.1 How much to trust each date

| Source of variation | Effect on Vimshottari dates | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Birth time ±15 min | about ∓24 days | A later birth moves every date earlier. The Moon moves about 0.5° per hour. |
| `time` vs `arc` balance | `arc` runs about 4–5 days earlier | `time` (Parashara's Light) uses the fraction of time the Moon spends in Chitra, 38.89%. `arc` (the textbook method) uses the fraction of the 13°20′ arc, 39.05%. |
| 365.2422 vs 365.25-day year | under 1 day before 2040 | The gap is only 0.0078 days per year. It matters only over many decades. |
| 360-day (savana) year | very large: Ju/Me would start Jan 2026 and Saturn MD Mar 2037 | Some software uses this. Treat it as a different school, not as an error bar. |

**Envelope rule:** the earliest date comes from `arc` with +15 min, and the latest from `time` with −15 min. That gives a window about seven weeks wide around each change. Things that depend only on the lagna sign, such as double transits and house-based gochara, hold from 43 minutes before to 66 minutes after the recorded time, so they are essentially fixed. Yogini dasha depends only on the Moon and moves only about ±3 days.

## 4.2 The setting: Jupiter mahadasha (Oct 2021 – Oct 2037)

For a Capricorn lagna, Jupiter rules the **3rd and 12th houses**. In BPHS (ch. 34, on Capricorn) Jupiter is listed with Mars and the Moon as a functional malefic, and Venus is the sole yogakaraka. That is the case against Jupiter. The case for it has three parts:

- Jupiter is a natural benefic placed in a kendra (the 4th, Aries). It does not suffer kendradhipati dosha, because it rules no kendra.
- Its aspects reach the 8th, the **10th (onto Mars)** and the 12th.
- It is conjunct the **lagna lord Saturn**. K.N. Rao and others read close association with the lagna lord as lifting a dusthana lord's results toward the self.

Jupiter sits in Ashwini, Ketu's nakshatra, and Ketu is in the lagna. On the star-lord principle (also used in KP), Jupiter therefore carries lagna significations as well. Its conjunction partner, Saturn, is **debilitated but has neecha-bhanga**: Mars, the lord of Aries, is in a kendra (the 10th) and occupies Libra, Saturn's exaltation sign, and Venus, the lord of Libra, is also in a kendra (the 7th).

Read this way, the mahadasha leans toward 3rd/12th-house matters: effort, writing, media, skills, siblings, foreign links, expenditure and retreat. These run through the 4th house (home, property, inner security) and look up at the career house. The period is less "Jupiterian luck" and more growth by effort. Its value shows in the antardashas of well-placed planets.

Phaladeepika (ch. 20) judges an antardasha from three things: (a) the antar lord's own lordship and dignity, (b) its position from the mahadasha lord, and (c) the transit at its start. Positions in kendra or trikona from the MD lord are favourable; positions 6th, 8th or 12th from it are mixed. Below, "from Ju" means counted from Aries.

### Antardashas through 2037

**Ju/Me · 2 Jun 2026 → 7 Sep 2028** *(band: 4 May – 26 Jun 2026 start)*
- **Natal promise:** Mercury rules the 9th and 6th. It is vargottama in the 7th with Venus, the 10th lord, and Rahu. The 9th and 10th lords together form a dharma-karma raja-yoga that leans toward partnership. Mercury is 4th from Jupiter, a kendra relation, so the texts read this as favourable.
- **Themes:** contracts, study, mentors, travel, and agreements that open doors. The 6th-lord side brings workload, competition and occasional disputes.
- **Sub-periods:** the current pratyantar is Me/Ke, running until 14 Nov 2026. It is followed by **Me/Ve, 14 Nov 2026 – 1 Apr 2027**, the yogakaraka's sub-period.
- **Transit overlay:** Jupiter is transiting exalted over natal Venus and Mercury in Cancer now (until 31 Oct 2026). Rahu then enters the lagna on 25 Nov 2026, and the half nodal return falls in late May to June 2027. The second year carries more self-redefinition than the first.

**Ju/Ke · 7 Sep 2028 → 13 Aug 2029** *(band: 9 Aug – 1 Oct 2028)*
- **Natal promise:** Ketu is in the lagna in Shravana, the Moon's star. "Kuja-vat Ketu" means it gives Mars-like results via its dispositor, the Saturn–Jupiter pair. Capricorn is 10th from Jupiter, a kendra.
- **Themes:** the Guru–Ketu pairing is classically linked to study, research, spiritual practice and pruning. Identity questions return, and some people simplify their lives in this phase.
- **Transit overlay:** this whole antardasha falls under Saturn's return in Aries (exact 29 Apr 2029). That is also *ashtama* Shani from the Moon. Expect consolidation, not expansion.

**Ju/Ve · 13 Aug 2029 → 13 Apr 2032** *(band: 16 Jul – 7 Sep 2029)*
- **Natal promise:** this is the most promising antardasha of the mahadasha. Venus is the **yogakaraka** (lord of the 5th and 10th) and the **Amatyakaraka**. It is in its own sign in D10 (Libra) and 4th from Jupiter.
- **Transit overlay:** this is the decade's strongest alignment. Saturn, the lagna lord, transits the 5th house (Taurus, 2030–32), and Jupiter passes through Scorpio, the 11th house, which has the chart's highest SAV score of 38. The 7th, 11th and 5th houses all receive double transits between 2030 and 2032.
- **Themes:** the texts associate a period like this with career elevation, creative output, romance and partnership commitments, and children-related decisions. Venus sits with Rahu, so the gains can come with appetite or overreach. Discernment matters.

**Ju/Su · 13 Apr 2032 → 31 Jan 2033** *(band: 16 Mar – 8 May 2032)*
- **Natal promise:** the Sun rules the 8th and is in the 6th, which forms Sarala, a viparita raja-yoga. It is 3rd from Jupiter, which is middling.
- **Themes:** authority, government, examinations, research and competitive situations. Gains come through overcoming obstacles.
- **Transit overlay:** Saturn enters Gemini on 30 May 2032 and transits the natal Sun in the 6th, a strong upachaya placement. Jupiter is debilitated in Capricorn over the lagna and Ketu. Results come from discipline more than ease.

**Ju/Mo · 31 Jan 2033 → 2 Jun 2034** *(band: 2 Jan – 24 Feb 2033)*
- **Natal promise:** the Moon is the 7th lord and the **Atmakaraka**. It is vargottama in the 9th. It is 6th from Jupiter, a 6/8 relation, so BPHS gives mixed results.
- **Transit overlay:** Rahu enters Virgo on 30 Jan 2033 and transits the natal Moon for almost the whole antardasha.
- **Themes:** spouse, partners, father and teachers, long journeys and higher learning are prominent. The emotional climate is intensified, and foreign or unconventional contacts appear. A good time for pilgrimage or study abroad. Emotional bandwidth needs care.

**Ju/Ma · 2 Jun 2034 → 9 May 2035** *(band: 4 May – 26 Jun 2034)*
- **Natal promise:** Mars rules the 4th and 11th and is in the 10th with directional strength. It is 7th from Jupiter, a kendra.
- **Themes:** drive in career, property and gains, and leadership claims.
- **Transit overlay:** Saturn enters Cancer on 12 Jul 2034. With Jupiter in Pisces, this gives a double transit on the 7th house (and on Virgo, the 7th lord's sign) from 14 Jul 2034 to 6 Apr 2035. Jupiter returns to Aries on 6 Apr 2035, with the exact return on 27 Apr 2035.

**Ju/Ra · 9 May 2035 → 1 Oct 2037** *(band: 10 Apr – 2 Jun 2035)*
- **Natal promise:** Rahu is in the 7th in Ashlesha, 4th from Jupiter. It gives results of its dispositor, the Moon (7th lord in the 9th), and of its conjunctions, Venus and Mercury. As the last antardasha of the mahadasha (*dasha-chidra*), the texts read it as a hand-over phase.
- **Transit overlay:** the nodal return falls on **19 Oct 2036** (true node; 27 Sep 2036 for the mean node). Sade Sati begins on **27 Aug 2036**.
- **Themes:** worldly ambition, foreign links and relationship intensity. Old structures dissolve so that Saturn's period can begin.

### Saturn mahadasha (Oct 2037 – Oct 2056), in outline

Saturn is the **lagna and 2nd lord**, so its period puts the chart's own agenda in charge.
- **Placement:** it is debilitated in the 4th with neecha-bhanga.
- **Vargas:** it is **exalted in D10 (Libra)** and in Virgo in D9.
- **Star lord:** it sits in Bharani, Venus's star, so it also delivers yogakaraka results.
- **Classical reading:** a strengthened lagna lord whose dasha starts slowly ("Old" avastha) and improves. The texts associate it with authority built through patience, property and family wealth (the 2nd), and work that shoulders responsibility.
- **Sade Sati overlap:** Sa/Sa (Oct 2037 – Oct 2040) runs during Sade Sati. Many commentators hold that Sade Sati weighs less when Saturn is the lagna lord. It is better read as a demanding apprenticeship than as misfortune.
- **Next antardasha:** Sa/Me (Oct 2040 – Jun 2043) brings back the 9th-lord and dharma themes.

## 4.5 Cross-checks from other dasha systems

**Yogini dasha.** The Moon is in Chitra, the 14th nakshatra: (14 + 3) mod 8 = 1, so the sequence starts with Mangala, followed by Pingala, Dhanya, Bhramari, Bhadrika, Ulka, Siddha and Sankata. The dates move only about ±3 days for ±15 min of birth time.
- **Siddha (Venus), 31 Jan 2020 – 31 Jan 2027:** a yogakaraka period, now in its last sub-period, Siddha/Ulka (Saturn).
- **Sankata (Rahu), 31 Jan 2027 – 31 Jan 2035:** "Sankata" means difficulty. Its first sub-period (to Nov 2028) agrees with Vimshottari that 2027–29 is pressured. Two sub-periods line up with the Ju/Ve peak in Vimshottari: Sankata/Dhanya (Jupiter, Jul 2029 – Mar 2030) and Sankata/Bhramari (Mars, Mar 2030 – Jan 2031). Sankata/Siddha (Venus, Jul 2033 – Jan 2035) supports the 2034 partnership window.
- **Later periods:** Mangala (Moon) runs 2035–36, Pingala (Sun) 2036–38, and **Dhanya (Jupiter) 2038–41**. The Dhanya period gives a softer undertone to the early Saturn MD and Sade Sati.

**Chara dasha (Jaimini).** This sign-based system is covered in depth in another chapter. Treat it as a third vote on the 2030–32 and 2034–35 windows, and remember that its starting sign depends on the lagna, which is robust for this chart.

## 4.6 Rectification by events: what Udai could provide

Each event below, with an approximate date (month and year is enough), would help narrow the birth time.

1. **Antardasha changes as felt.** Past boundaries at the recorded time:
   - Ra/Ve: Apr 2015 · Ra/Su: Apr 2018 · Ra/Mo: Mar 2019 · Ra/Ma: Sep 2020
   - Jupiter MD: **1 Oct 2021** · Ju/Sa: Nov 2023 · Ju/Me: **2 Jun 2026**

   If a clear turn in life happened, for example around Sep 2021 or May 2026, that points to a slightly later birth. A turn around late Oct 2021 or late Jun 2026 points to an earlier one. Each 24-day shift corresponds to about 15 minutes. Reading the pratyantar sub-periods sharpens this further.
2. **D9 lagna, for relationships and marriage.** The D9 lagna is Aries only from 21:29:30 to 21:42. Before that it is Pisces; after 21:42 it is Taurus, and Gemini after 21:54.
   - Under the Aries option, the D9 7th is Libra, ruled by Venus.
   - Under the Taurus option, it is Scorpio, ruled by Mars.

   The dates of a serious relationship, engagement or marriage, and the partner's temperament, help decide between them: the date is tested against the D9 7th lord's dasha and the double transits.
3. **D10 lagna, for career.** The D10 lagna holds Sagittarius only from about 21:26 to 21:37, which is extremely sensitive. It is Scorpio earlier and Capricorn later. The useful inputs are the dates of a first job, promotions, switches or starting a business, and the field of work. Sagittarius suggests teaching, advisory or law-flavoured work; Capricorn suggests administrative or engineering work.
4. **D12, for parents.** The D12 lagna is Taurus from about 21:29:30 to 21:39. It is Aries or Pisces earlier and Gemini later. Major milestones in the parents' lives are tested against the D12 4th (mother) and 9th (father) houses and their lords' dashas. Examples: relocations, career peaks, retirements, health episodes.
5. **KP ruling planets.** The lagna's KP sub-lord changes every few minutes: Moon/Mars at the recorded time, Moon/Moon at −6 min, Moon/Rahu at +2 min, Moon/Jupiter at +9 min. Udai would need to note the ruling planets (day lord, Moon star and sub, lagna star and sub) at the exact moment of a significant question, or at a few well-dated events. The candidate sub-lord that recurs is kept.
6. **Siblings and moves.** The D3 lagna holds Taurus until +31 min, so it is only a coarse check. The D4 lagna (Aries until +13 min) is useful against the dates of property purchases or house moves.

The best inputs are three to five dated events from different areas of life, such as education, first job, relationship, a move and a parent's milestone. With those, the ±15 min band can usually be narrowed to about ±3 minutes.
