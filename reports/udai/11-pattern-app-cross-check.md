# Cross-check: Pattern app "Taking Stock" (23 Jun 2026 – 24 May 2027)

## What the event is (fact)
- **Transiting North Node conjunct natal South Node.** This is the nodal reversal, or half nodal return, and it recurs every 18.6 years (around ages 9, 27–28 and 46).
- **Exact on 24 May 2027** (tropical true node over natal South Node at 13°56′ Aquarius), matching Pattern's end date.
  - 3° orb: 25 Apr – 16 Sep 2027.
  - 5° orb: 7 Apr – 14 Oct 2027.
- **Our Vedic timing chapter (04) flagged the same event independently** as the "half nodal return", 21 May 2027 (sidereal true node). Rahu enters the Capricorn lagna on 25 Nov 2026.
- **It lands on the 1st/7th axis in both zodiacs**: tropical Aquarius 1st (Asc 5°20′), and the sidereal Capricorn lagna with Ketu crossing the natal Venus–Mercury–Rahu cluster in the 7th.

## What the text is (assessment under GROUND-RULES)
- **Mostly generic.** Lines like "take stock", "change what's not working: career, relationships, living situation, behaviours, beliefs" and "if you don't like it, change it" would read as true for nearly anyone (the Barnum effect).
- **The specific, tradition-backed parts:**
  - The node over the 1st: identity redefinition, ambition and reinvention. Its shadow is restlessness and over-identifying with a role.
  - Ketu over the 7th, where Venus (yogakaraka/AmK) and Mercury sit: detachment from, re-examination of, or clarification of partnerships, collaborations and contracts.
