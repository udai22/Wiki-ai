## 1. How to read this: three layers of certainty

The birth time may be a few minutes off, and chart factors move at very different speeds. So every statement here is sorted by how much birth-time error it can survive.

| Layer | Holds if the true time is… | What lives here |
|---|---|---|
| **Robust** | 21:05 – 22:41 (lagna Capricorn, −43 to +66 min) | Houses, lordships, yogas, aspects, the Moon and its nakshatra, karakas, karakamsa, Arudha Lagna and Upapada, ashtakavarga, Chara dasha sequence, Yogini dasha, Nadi combinations, double transits |
| **Moderate** | dates slide but don't break | Vimshottari dates (≈ 3 weeks per 15 min), lagna nakshatra (Shravana from 21:29:30), Hellenistic Lots |
| **Time-sensitive** | only a few minutes | Navamsa (D9) lagna, D10 career lagna, D12, D60, KP cusp sub-lords |

Osho made a similar three-way split: the essential (fixed), the semi-essential (changeable with awareness), and the superficial (fortune-telling). The robust layer is your fixed signature. The fragile layer is where readers most often overreach.

## 2. The robust core: where every school agrees

### 2.1 Saturn is the axis of the life
- **Vedic:** Capricorn rising, so Saturn rules both the 1st and 2nd houses. It sits in the 4th within half a degree of its deepest debilitation point (Aries 20°), with Jupiter.
- **Western:** Saturn rules the rising sign (Aquarius tropically) in every frame tested.
- **Saturn's debilitation is cancelled several times over.** Five or six classical neecha-bhanga conditions apply (Phaladeepika ch. 7; Jataka Parijata):
  - Mars, lord of Aries, is in a kendra (10th).
  - Venus, lord of Saturn's exaltation sign, is in a kendra (7th).
  - The Sun, which exalts in Aries, is 10th from the Moon.
  - Mars and Venus are in mutual kendras.
  - Mars aspects Saturn.
  - Saturn is exalted in the D10.
- **Where the masters split:**
  - Phaladeepika and Jataka Parijata call this *neecha-bhanga raja yoga*.
  - Varahamihira has no such doctrine.
  - Raman and K.N. Rao read it as *strength reached through an early setback*. In their reading it gives its full results in Saturn's own period, which starts around **Oct 2037**.
- **The shared picture:** competence that is built slowly and earned, not given. Every school ties the deepest maturation to the late 2020s (Saturn return) and the late 2030s (Saturn mahadasha).

### 2.2 Venus is the engine (yogakaraka + Amatyakaraka)
- **Parashari:** Venus rules the 5th and 10th, making it the single yogakaraka for Capricorn. It sits in the 7th with Mercury, lord of the 9th. That gives a **dharma-karmadhipati raja yoga**.
- **Jaimini:** Venus is also the Amatyakaraka, the career significator.
- **Divisional charts:** in the D10, Venus is in its own sign (Libra), together with exalted Saturn and Mars.
- **The caveat:** Rahu is 1°44′ from Venus, and both are in Ashlesha.
  - Frawley reads Ashlesha's power as penetrating and binding.
  - Sutton reads it as serpent wisdom.
  - The masters disagree on whether Rahu *spoils* the yoga or *amplifies* it in unconventional, foreign or technological directions.
- **The shared picture:** career, partnership and creative output are fused. Gains come through other people, contracts and design-like work.

### 2.3 The Moon is the soul (Atmakaraka), and Chitra is the signature
- The Moon in Virgo, Chitra pada 2, is vargottama and holds through the entire ±90-minute window.
- It is the Atmakaraka, so the karakamsa is **Virgo**, with Saturn in it.
- The Moon and Mercury exchange signs (Moon in Virgo, Mercury in Cancer). This is a 7th–9th lord parivartana, often overlooked and classically auspicious.
- Mars is also in Chitra, its own nakshatra, in the 10th with full directional strength (dig bala).
- **The shared picture:** a maker, designer or analyst. Chitra's deity is Tvashtar, the divine craftsman, and its power is "gathering merit through crafted work". Its shadow is perfectionism and restlessness.
- **Western echo:** the Moon is the sect light of this night chart, in the 9th with the Lot of Fortune.

### 2.4 Other robust findings
- **Ashtakavarga:** Scorpio, the 11th house of gains, scores **38**, the chart's peak. The 9th and 8th score 24 each, so fortune comes through effort and timing more than windfall.
- **Arudha Lagna in Aries** (with Jupiter and Saturn): the public image is a principled initiator carrying gravitas. It contrasts with the more reserved Capricorn self, which has Ketu on the lagna.
  - 7 of 9 planets are in kendras from the AL, which makes it a highly visible chart.
- **Upapada in Leo**, trine to the AL, with Jupiter aspecting it. This is a supportive marriage indicator in the Jaimini reading.
- **Yogas absent but often claimed:** Gaja Kesari (Jupiter is 8th from the Moon), all five Mahapurusha yogas, and Budha-Aditya. **Kemadruma is also absent**, which is good.
- **Nadi** (sign-based, needs no birth time): Jupiter (the self) sits with Saturn (karma) and opposite Mars. That reads as a responsibility-heavy life story with technical-plus-advisory standing.
- **Tara bala:** whichever nakshatra the lagna is in (Shravana or Uttara Ashadha), it is a friendly star from the Moon's.

## 5. Where the masters disagree

Each disagreement and how it affects this chart:

- **Rahu's aspects** (some give it the 5th and 9th): this decides whether Rahu also touches the 11th and 3rd houses. Minor.
- **Neecha-bhanga raja yoga** (Phaladeepika) vs. "cancelled weakness" (Raman, Rao): this sets how much to expect from Saturn. The consensus is "late but real".
- **Jupiter as functional malefic** (lord of the 3rd and 12th, BPHS ch. 34) vs. benefic in a kendra aspecting the 10th: this decides the tone of the 2021–37 mahadasha. The chapters read it as **growth through effort**.
- **The 7- vs 8-karaka scheme:** it changes only the labels for parents and children. AK, AmK and BK are the same either way.
- **The Arudha exception rule:** with it, the AL is Aries (Parashara's Light uses this); without it, Cancer. All 12 arudhas on your screen match the exception.
- **Chara dasha variants** (Scorpio's co-lord, Rao vs. SJC counting): these change which sign is "now" (Cancer, Libra or Leo). Every variant agrees that **Libra (career) and Cancer (partnership)** dominate 2013–2045.
- **Sidereal vs. tropical:**
  - Saturn is debilitated sidereally, comfortable tropically (Taurus).
  - The Sun is in Gemini sidereally, Cancer 0°48′ tropically.
  - Both systems are internally consistent. The Saturn-centred story survives in both.
- **The Vimshottari "time" vs "arc" method:** about 4 days' difference. Parashara's Light uses "time".

## 6. Narrowing the birth time (rectification)

Three to five dated events from different areas of life should narrow the time to about ±3 minutes. A month and year is enough for each. Useful ones:

1. **Turning points around Sep–Oct 2021 and May–Jun 2026** (the Jupiter MD and Ju/Me boundaries). An early turn suggests a later birth; a late turn suggests an earlier one.
2. **Career:** first job, switches, promotions, business start, and the field of work. These test the Sagittarius vs Capricorn D10.
3. **Relationships:** serious relationships, engagement or marriage, and the partner's temperament. These test the Aries vs Taurus D9.
4. **Parents' milestones:** moves, career peaks, retirements. These test the D12.
5. **Home moves and property:** tests the D4 (Aries until +13 min).

## 7. How to hold all this

- **Paracelsus:** *sapiens dominabitur astris*, the wise person rules their stars. The chart shows inclinations and timing, not commands.
- **Osho:** the robust layer is the given. Awareness works on the middle layer, and the fragile layer is where prediction becomes guesswork.
- **Evidence:** controlled studies have not supported astrology's predictive claims (see [knowledge/01](../../knowledge/01-how-astrology-works.md)). This map is an accurate account of what the traditions say about this sky. It is not a scientific forecast.
